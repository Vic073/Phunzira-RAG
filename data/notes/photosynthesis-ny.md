Photosynthesis ndi njira imene zomera zobiriwira zimapangira chakudya chawo. Zomera zimagwiritsa ntchito kuwala kwa dzuwa, madzi ndi mpweya wa carbon dioxide kuti zipange shuga ndi mpweya wa okosijeni. Chomera chimatenga madzi kudzera m'mizu yake, ndipo chimatenga mpweya wa carbon dioxide kuchokera mumlengalenga kudzera m'timabowo tating'ono m'masamba. Chlorophyll, chinthu chobiriwira chomwe chili m'masamba, chimatenga mphamvu kuchokera ku kuwala kwa dzuwa. Shuga amene amapangidwa amadyetsa chomera, ndipo mpweya wa okosijeni umatulutsidwa mumlengalenga. Popanda photosynthesis, zamoyo zambiri sizikanakhala ndi chakudya kapena mpweya wopumira.
