title: The water cycle
lang: en
---
The water cycle describes how water moves between the Earth's surface and the atmosphere. The heat of the sun causes water in lakes, rivers and the ocean to evaporate and rise as water vapour. High in the air the vapour cools and condenses into tiny droplets that form clouds. When the droplets grow heavy they fall as rain. Rain flows into rivers and lakes or soaks into the soil, and the cycle begins again.
