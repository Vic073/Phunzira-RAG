title: Photosynthesis
lang: en
---
Photosynthesis is the process by which green plants make their own food. Plants use sunlight, water and carbon dioxide to make sugar and oxygen. The plant takes in water through its roots and carbon dioxide from the air through tiny holes in its leaves. Chlorophyll, the green substance in the leaves, captures energy from sunlight. The sugar made during photosynthesis feeds the plant, and the oxygen is released into the air. Without photosynthesis most living things would have no food and no oxygen to breathe.
