title: Lake Malawi
lang: en
---
Lake Malawi is the third largest lake in Africa and borders Malawi, Mozambique and Tanzania. The lake is about 580 kilometres long and holds more species of fish than any other lake in the world, mostly colourful cichlids known locally as mbuna. Many Malawians depend on the lake for food and income through fishing. The lake also supplies water for drinking and irrigation, and it is a major attraction for tourists.
