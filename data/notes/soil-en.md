title: Soil and soil conservation
lang: en
---
Soil is the top layer of the ground where plants grow. It is made of tiny pieces of rock, dead plant and animal matter, air and water. Fertile soil contains nutrients that plants need to grow. Farmers in Malawi add manure or fertiliser to restore nutrients, and they rotate crops such as maize and groundnuts to keep the soil healthy. Soil erosion happens when heavy rain or wind carries the top layer away, so planting trees and building ridges help to protect it.
