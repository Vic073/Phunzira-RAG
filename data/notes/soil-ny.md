Nthaka ndi gawo la pamwamba pa dziko pomwe zomera zimamera. Imapangidwa ndi tizidutswa ting'onoting'ono ta miyala, zotsalira za zomera ndi nyama zakufa, mpweya ndi madzi. Nthaka yachonde imakhala ndi zakudya zomwe zomera zimafunikira kuti zikule. Alimi ku Malawi amathira manyowa kapena feteleza kuti nthaka ibwezeretse zakudya zake, ndipo amasinthasintha mbewu monga chimanga ndi mtedza kuti nthaka ikhale yathanzi. Kukokoloka kwa nthaka kumachitika pamene mvula yamphamvu kapena mphepo itenga gawo la pamwamba, choncho kubzala mitengo ndi kupanga makwawa kumathandiza kuiteteza.
